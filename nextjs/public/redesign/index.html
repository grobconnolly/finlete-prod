Questions people ask before investing

The short answers. Each offering's documents have the long ones.

Can I actually make money?

Only if he earns enough in the majors. You receive a fixed percentage of his pre-tax, on-field MLB income for 25 years, so a short or low-paid MLB career can pay back less than you invested. In the simulation above, break-even is roughly $12M of career MLB earnings, and the figure varies by offering. If he never reaches the majors there is nothing to pay out and you lose your entire investment. Treat it as a speculative, long-term position.

What exactly am I buying?

Shares issued by Finlete Funding, Inc. under Regulation Crowdfunding. Finlete holds a contract for a percentage of the athlete's pre-tax, on-field MLB income and pays it out to shareholders as dividends. Your investment is in those shares, not a direct contract with the athlete. Each offering's Form C describes the exact security.

What happens if the athlete doesn't make it?

Payouts come only from Major League earnings. If he retires or is released before earning at that level, investors receive no payouts. The athlete does not owe the money back.

How does this help the athlete?

Upfront capital pays for training, nutrition and family support without debt, and he owes nothing back if he never reaches the majors. In Yairo Padilla's words: “The money I receive here will help me focus on baseball and be the best I can be. And if I make it, we will celebrate together.”

Can I sell my shares?

Shares sold under Regulation Crowdfunding are subject to a one-year lockup. During that period they may only be transferred in limited circumstances, typically to the issuing company, an accredited investor or a family member. After the lockup the shares are still not publicly traded and there is currently no marketplace for them, so expect to hold for the full term. Your return comes from payouts, not from reselling.

What if an offering doesn't reach its funding target?

If the minimum funding target is not reached, all funds are returned to investors after the close of the offering.

What if I change my mind?

You can cancel your investment at any time, for any reason, until 48 hours prior to a closing. If your funds are already in escrow, they will be promptly refunded. To cancel, email info@dealmakersecurities.com.
