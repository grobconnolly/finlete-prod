Questions people ask before investing

The short answers. Each offering's documents have the long ones.

Can I actually make money?

Yes, if the athlete earns income at the Major League level. Investors receive annual payouts based on his total pre-tax, on-field MLB income during the 25-year term. If he never reaches the majors there is nothing to pay out, and you can lose your entire investment. Treat it as a speculative, long-term position.

What happens if the athlete doesn't make it?

Payouts come only from Major League earnings. If he retires or is released before earning at that level, investors receive no payouts. The athlete does not owe the money back.

How does this help the athlete?

Most aspiring pro athletes earn modest salaries and have no long-term financial guarantees. Upfront cash can mean better training, nutrition, support for family and peace of mind, without taking on debt.

Can I sell my shares?

Shares sold under Regulation Crowdfunding are subject to a one-year lockup. During that period they may only be transferred in limited circumstances, typically to the issuing company, an accredited investor or a family member.

What if an offering doesn't reach its funding target?

If the minimum funding target is not reached, all funds are returned to investors after the close of the offering.

What if I change my mind?

You can cancel your investment at any time, for any reason, until 48 hours prior to a closing. If your funds are already in escrow, they will be promptly refunded.

Are you a player?

Finlete helps you secure upfront funding, build a loyal fan base and stay focused on your sport. No debt, and nothing to repay if you don't reach the majors.
