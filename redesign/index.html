What you're actually buying

Each athlete signs a future-earnings agreement with Finlete. He receives money now for training and living costs, and in return commits a fixed percentage of his future baseball salary to the pool his investors own. It is not a loan he has to repay, and it is not equity in a company.

You buy shares in that pool. If he reaches the majors, Finlete collects the agreed percentage from his contract and pays it out to shareholders in proportion to the shares they hold. If he never signs a Major League contract, there is nothing to distribute and you can lose your whole investment.

Every offering publishes its Form C and offering circular. Read them before you invest.

Questions people ask before investing

The short answers. Each offering's documents have the long ones.

Can I actually make money?

Yes, if the athlete signs a Major League contract. You're then paid your share of the agreed percentage of his salary, distributed twice a year. If he never reaches the majors there is nothing to distribute, and you can lose your entire investment. Treat this as a speculative, long-term position.

What happens if the athlete doesn't make it?

The agreement only pays out on Major League earnings. If he retires or is released before signing an MLB contract, shareholders receive no distributions. The athlete does not owe the money back.

Can I sell my shares?

Shares sold under Regulation Crowdfunding are locked up for one year. During that period they may only be transferred in limited circumstances, typically to the issuer, an accredited investor or a family member. There is no public market for them after that.

What if the raise doesn't reach its target?

If the minimum funding target is not reached, all funds are returned to investors after the offering closes.

Can I change my mind?

You can cancel for any reason until 48 hours before a closing. If your funds are already in escrow they are refunded promptly.

How does this help the athlete?

Minor-league pay is low and the road to the majors is long. The money raised covers training, nutrition, housing and travel now, when it matters most, without taking on debt.

Are you a player?

Finlete raises upfront money for your career from fans who want to see you make it. You keep playing. We handle the offering, the compliance and the investor community.
